TIKTOK &EU
• In the European Union, TikTok has been the subject of 

investigations and legal action over concerns about 
data security and influence in electoral processes.

• To address data security concerns, TikTok 
implemented "Project Clover", working with NCC 
Group, an independent cybersecurity firm, to monitor 
data flows and ensure that only authorized employees 
have access to them. The company says it has not and 
will not pass data to Chinese authorities, even if asked 
to do so.

• In December 2024, the European Commission 
launched an investigation into TikTok, suspecting that 
the platform was used to influence Romania's 
presidential election. It is alleged that foreign actors 
used TikTok to promote a pro-Russian candidate, 
which led to the annulment of the first round of 
voting by Romania's Constitutional Court. The 
investigation focuses on how TikTok manages the risks 
of content manipulation and policies on political 
advertising to determine whether the platform 
violated the EU's Digital Services Regulation.
